Why we chant

The holy name, offered together

Chanting the Hare Krishna maha-mantra on beads is a practice open to everyone, regardless of age or experience. Each round of 108 beads is an offering of 1,728 holy names — and every offering, however small, joins the rising count of this community's yagna.

“harer nāma harer nāma harer nāmaiva kevalam — in this age of quarrel and hypocrisy, the only means of deliverance is chanting the holy name of the Lord. There is no other way.”

Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta · Ādi-līlā 17.21
